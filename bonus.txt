Lab 6 - Bonus

Question:
Which counties outside Illinois contribute the largest net migration gains
to Cook County?

Answer:
Jefferson County, Kentucky, has the largest estimated net gain: 433 people.
The data shows 637 people moving into Cook County from Jefferson County
and 204 moving the other way. The difference is 433.

The 10 counties shown on the map are:

County and state                      Net gain to Cook County
Jefferson County, Kentucky                        433
St. Joseph County, Indiana                        359
Baltimore City, Maryland                          346
Prince George's County, Maryland                  330
Hartford County, Connecticut                      297
Travis County, Texas                              251
New Haven County, Connecticut                     230
Canyon County, Idaho                              225
El Paso County, Texas                             218
Stanislaus County, California                     207

Together, these counties have an estimated net gain of 2,896 people to Cook
County. This total covers only the 10 selected county pairs.

The selection keeps counties outside Illinois with a positive Net_B2A value,
sorts from largest to smallest, and takes the first 10. Net_B2A is inflow
minus outflow. York County, South Carolina, also has a value of 207;
Stanislaus County was selected using the smaller county GEOID to break the tie.

Thicker, darker lines show larger net gains. Hover over a circle for its label.
